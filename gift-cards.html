PlayStation gift cards FAQ

Some of the most common questions for purchasing and using PlayStation Store gift cards.

Can I gift games on PlayStation?

Yes - PlayStation Store gift cards are the go-to way of gifting digital games on PS5, PS5 Pro and PS4.

Why buy a PlayStation Store gift card?

PlayStation Store gift cards make the perfect birthday and holiday gifts for gamers. A gift card lets them choose anything available at PlayStation Store, including games, add-ons and PlayStation Plus.

Can I use a PlayStation Store gift card to buy digital games on sale?

Totally! Spending PlayStation Store credit on one of our many epic promotions like Black Friday or Holiday Sale is a great way to get more bang for your buck.

Can I gift Fortnite V-Bucks on PlayStation?

Digital currencies like V-Bucks, EA SPORTS FC Points and Call of Duty® Points can absolutely be purchased with a PlayStation Store gift card.

How do I use a PlayStation gift card?

You can redeem your gift card at PlayStation Store on your PS5 or PS4 console, PlayStation App, or web browser. Sign in and select your profile, followed by Redeem Code. Just enter the 12-digit code and you’re ready to go.

How do I send a PlayStation gift card to a friend or relative?

Choose a printable PDF – perfect to pop in a card – or digital voucher to send via email once you’ve decided on a custom design and message.

What can I buy with a PlayStation gift card?

If it's a digital item available on PlayStation Store, you can purchase it with a gift card. That includes PlayStation Plus memberships, digital game add-ons, virtual currency and digital pre-orders.

Where can I buy a PlayStation gift card?

You can find a list of retailers above on this page, or purchase PlayStation Store gift cards from many high-street retailers and online stores.

Can I give PlayStation Plus with a gift card?

Yes - the recipient can use the pre-loaded credit to purchase any PlayStation Plus membership they like.

Do PlayStation gift cards have to be activated?

Some voucher codes require activation at the time of purchase. This is clearly visible on the front and back of the card.

Can I buy PlayStation consoles, accessories and merch with a gift card?

PlayStation Store gift cards can only be used on digital products at PlayStation Store. Physical products and merch can’t be purchased with a gift card.
